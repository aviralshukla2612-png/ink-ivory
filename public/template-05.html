Our Story

Every forever begins somewhere. Here are a few moments that brought our journey together.

📱

One match away

from forever

A little swipe, a matrimony match, and two cities suddenly felt a little closer. Chennai met Bengaluru, and a simple hello began a story neither had planned but both were ready to discover.

📱

A message that stayed

hello, from a screen

A first message turned into easy conversation, and a smile every time the phone lit up.

✈️

Chennai to Bengaluru

one call at a time

Different cities, busy days, and conversations that never seemed long enough. With every call and every shared laugh, the miles mattered a little less.

☕

Oh Manapenne!

a first hello

A warm welcome, a little nervousness, filter coffee, and smiles that said more than words. The first meeting made the possibility of a shared future feel wonderfully real.

forever begins

One journey, two families, and countless little moments still to come. We can't wait to begin this new chapter surrounded by the people we love.
